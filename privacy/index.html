Who we are

Sidestick PRO is run by MB EPINI, a small partnership registered in Lithuania (company code 307536669, VAT number LT100019451612), Viršilų g. 6-403, Vilnius, Lithuania. We are the data controller for the Sidestick PRO app and this website. Contact: support@sidestick.pro.

Without an account

You can use the free trial questions without an account. Then nothing about you is stored on our side, and nothing is sent to RevenueCat or to any sign-in provider. Your progress stays on your device.

What we store when you have an account

  • your email address and the sign-in method you used (Apple, Google, Facebook Limited Login, or an emailed code);
  • your progress: finished tests and exams and the answers you gave;
  • your flagged questions and your notes;
  • the identifier of your one active device;
  • your purchase status: whether you have full access, and until when.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To create your account, sign you in, keep your progress, flags and notes in step across your devices, and give you the access you have bought: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent account sharing, and answer your support requests: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep accounting records of purchases: a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase keeps account and progress data, in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Resend (EU) sends the sign-in emails.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases and, if you choose it, Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat (a processor based in the United States) checks and records your subscription. It is used only after you sign in. It then receives your account identifier and your purchase data, and nothing else about you.
  • Google and Facebook (Meta) are used only if you choose them to sign in. The Facebook SDK runs with automatic events, advertiser ID and metadata collection switched off.

The app has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics software.

Transfers outside the EU

RevenueCat, Apple, Google and Meta may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient takes part in it. Ask us if you want details.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in server logs. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how it does so. We do not receive or use those logs.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings, then Delete account. This removes your account and all the data we hold for it on our servers, and we also delete your customer record at RevenueCat. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that separately in your Apple ID settings.

You can also ask us by email at support@sidestick.pro. Write from the address on the account and tell us which sign-in method you used. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, you may write from any address: tell us you used Hide My Email, and we will ask you to confirm in the app that the account is yours before we delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete the account. Records we must keep by law, such as accounting records of purchases, are kept for the period the law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to support@sidestick.pro. You may also complain to the supervisory authority in Lithuania, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change is important.
