Support

Write to support@sidestick.pro. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Report a wrong question

Use the report button on the question screen in the app. It reaches us privately, with the question already attached.

Subscription and refunds

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. To cancel or change one, open your Apple Account settings, then Subscriptions. For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. In the app, Restore purchases brings your access back on a new device.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings, then Delete account. Or write to us from the account's email address. Deleting your account in the Privacy Policy explains the rest, including Hide My Email.

Sign-in trouble

Tell us the sign-in method you used (Apple, Google, Facebook or emailed code) and what the app showed.
